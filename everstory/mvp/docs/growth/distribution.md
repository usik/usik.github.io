## Measurement and stagnation rule

Report KST completed days. Separate pageviews, daily estimated visitors, character selections, chatting accounts and signup counts. IP+browser daily hashing estimates devices/visitors; it is not exact people, cross-day identity or perfect bot filtering. Operator exclusion requires recognized sign-in; anonymous owner visits cannot reliably be separated. DNT/GPC and blockers reduce collection.

After three complete measured days, all-zero external visitors is a trigger to prepare one new channel test. With six complete days, compare the sum of daily external estimated visitors for the latest three days to the preceding three; a flat/decreasing sum is a stagnation signal. These sums are visitor-days, not unique people. Never trigger from missing data or incomplete days. Change one channel or creative at a time; log the published URL, time, account, source code and observed result. Do not repost the same channel daily.

Routine daily automation currently reports the signal and prepares a channel-specific draft. Actual publication requires an available connection with the user's intended account and the channel's rules satisfied. Report a connection blocker honestly rather than saying a draft was published.
